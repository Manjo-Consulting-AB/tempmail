# Let your favourite AI manage your inbox

We are all busy doing way more important things than scrolling through hundreds of completely useless emails to find the few that actually need some kind of attention, whether it's that webinar sign-up or the email from Granny wishing you a happy birthday.

To deal with that, we have now hooked up an MCP server that lets you connect your favourite AI agent to your Mail Shield inbox. The agent can check your emails and let you know if any of them need action. It can also create both Timed and Sticky addresses for you, and of course delete them when asked to. Why you would need that is entirely up to you to figure out, but we find it perfect when running automated code and system tests.

## Give it access

To let the MCP log in to your account, head to your profile settings and scroll all the way down to the Security section and "Connected apps". From there, just follow the instructions and you'll be up and chatting with your inbox in no time.

Connecting an agent is a Pro feature, so if you're on your Pro trial you can try it right now at no cost.

## Things to ask it

Not sure where to start? Here are a few prompts to get you going:

- "Do I have anything in my inbox that needs an answer?"
- "Create a Timed address I can use for a newsletter sign-up."
- "Summarise this week's offers in my ads inbox."
- "Which of my Sticky addresses got the most mail this week?"

## Don't worry, it won't run wild

Letting an AI loose in your mail can feel a bit scary, so we've built in some guardrails. When you connect an app you choose what it gets: read-only access, or read and change. With read-only access it can look but never touch. And even with full access it can't delete an address by accident: it has to explicitly confirm the deletion, after being told exactly which address would go, along with all of its mail. The same goes for replacing your Timed address. We also tell the agent that the mail it reads is just data from strangers, never instructions to follow, which makes it a lot harder for a sneaky email to boss your agent around.

## What else can it be used for?

Besides using Timed addresses for testing, I let one of my Claude agents check my inbox on a schedule, mining my "ads" inbox for offers from the local stores. From those offers the agent picks out the stuff I like and builds the menu of the week. I then approve or adjust the menu before my shopping list is updated.

That approval step is there for a reason. Once, the agent mistook a small green power drill from Lidl for a vegetable and happily put it on the menu, to be chopped up and tossed into the salad. Luckily, I was the one doing the shopping that week.

You can just as easily ask the agent to check your emails for offers from your favourite geek store or the coffee joint around the corner.

Try it out and build something fun (or useful)!
