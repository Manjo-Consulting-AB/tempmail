# Hook it up

If you've poked around in the Pro settings, you already know about webhooks and that you can use them to notify external services like Pushover, Zapier, or whatever else you like to hook it up with.

Previously, I mainly used webhooks with Pushover to get a ping whenever something hit one of my mailboxes. This got super annoying pretty quickly when I tossed some of my more talkative senders into MailShield. Heck, my Pushover app started sounding more like an arcade from the '60s than a notification app. The result? I stopped using Pushover for notifications.

But it kept bugging me that those pesky senders had made me stop using a feature I liked and actually kind of needed. So I got cracking and copied the foundation of the RSS feature I mentioned a few days ago.

So now you can use webhooks on specific addresses instead of having them fire for everything.

To enable the feature, just configure a webhook of your choice and the "Hook" button will appear next to your sticky addresses.

Happy hooking!
