# RSS Feed

I'm lazy when it comes to information — I prefer it in a format that's easy to consume and doesn't interrupt my day.

That's why Mail Shield now offers an RSS feed for users with a Pro account. To enable it, log in to your account, go to Settings, and open the RSS section.

![RSS Feed](blog/images/rss-feed.jpg)

Copy the feed URL and add it to your favourite RSS reader to access the emails in your Mail Shield account.

Update: Since mid september 26 I now use NetNewsWire to read my RSS feeds, have to say its pretty good (and free)...

Read a book not emails!
