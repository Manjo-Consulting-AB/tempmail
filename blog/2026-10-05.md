# Sometimes you have to take a break

We all need a break from the world every now and then. It could be a few weeks somewhere nice with the family, or it could be sailing across the Atlantic. Whatever we do, the world keeps spinning while we're away, and so does your Mail Shield inbox.

Until now, if you stayed away for more than a week, the mail that arrived while you were off doing the things you deserve, undisturbed, was gone, cleared out by Mail Shield's automatic cleanup. That left you with two options: check your inbox while you're away, or lose the emails that arrive while you're gone.

Not anymore. If you've been using Mail Shield for a while, I know this release is something you've been both asking, and waiting for.

![Pause your emails](blog/images/take_a_break.jpeg)

## Put your mail on hold

With the new retention hold, the mail on your Sticky addresses can stay for 30 days instead of the usual maximum of 7. That's enough time to cross the Atlantic in a sailboat, or to disappear far enough off the grid that you actually start looking forward to getting back online.

To start a hold, open settings in your profile, find **Default lifetime** and choose **30 days**. While the hold is active, every email on your Sticky addresses is kept for 30 days from the day it arrived, including the mail that was already waiting when you started the hold. Timed addresses still expire on schedule, just like before.

Retention holds are a Pro feature, so if you're on your Pro trial you can try one right now.

## Use them wisely

Mail Shield isn't meant to be an ever-growing inbox full of "I'll look into that later", so you get 4 holds per rolling year. If you change your mind and end a hold early, it still counts as one of your 4.
